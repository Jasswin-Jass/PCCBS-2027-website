Legacy

A Century of Charity through Education

PSG & Sons' Charities

“Let there be charity so that other people may share my family's prosperity” Sri. P. S. Govindaswamy Naidu, the benevolent philanthropist

He firmly believed that “Charity begins at home!”. True to his words, the four praiseworthy sons of Sri. P. S. Govindaswamy Naidu founded the PSG & Sons' Charities Trust in 1926, to take his vision forward. Through the Trust they took “Charity through Education” with great missionary zeal. The Trust extends its influence to over 30,000 students across 30 educational institutions, offering a comprehensive array of educational opportunities.

PSG College of Arts & Science

PSG College of Arts & Science is a premier institution started in the year 1947 by the PSG & Sons' Charities, Coimbatore. The College has been granted Autonomous Status by the UGC since 1978, and has been accredited with A++ Grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (4th Cycle) and has an ISO 9001:2015 Certification from TUV - Rheinland, Germany. It is identified as a “College with Potential for Excellence” by the University Grants Commission, New Delhi. Ranked 10th under NIRF 2025 and is the recipient of many National and International awards and recognitions. The College offers 49 Undergraduate programmes, 32 Postgraduate, 5 years Integrated Postgraduate (MSc Software Systems) and 4 PG Diploma Programmes. PhD Research Programme are offered in 25 disciplines. The College has a long-standing academic tradition of excellence in education and research.

The Conference

About the Conference

Central focus of the Conference

Integrating AI, Sustainable Science, and Translational Research for a Knowledge – Driven Viksit Bharat 2047.

The International Conference on Research Advances and AI - Driven Innovation in Physico - Chemical, Computational and Biological Sciences is a global interdisciplinary platform that brings together researchers, academicians, industry experts, innovators, and students to explore cutting edge scientific advancements.

The conference emphasizes the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in accelerating discoveries across physical, chemical, computational, and life sciences. It aims to foster collaborative research, sustainable technologies, precision healthcare, and data - driven innovation aligned with the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.

The conference aims to promote knowledge exchange, interdisciplinary partnerships, and the translation of scientific research into real world societal impact through the keynote lectures, technical sessions, workshops, and poster presentations.
